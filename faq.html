Common Questions

General Information

Do I need any prior cooking experience?

Not at all! We offer classes designed for all skill levels. Our "Beginner Basics" series is perfect for those who have never held a chef's knife, while our "Masterclass" series is tailored for experienced home cooks looking to elevate their techniques.

What should I wear to a class?

For safety reasons, we strictly require closed-toe shoes with non-slip soles. We recommend wearing comfortable clothing that you don't mind getting a little messy. We will provide professional aprons and side towels for you to use during the session.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions or allergies?

Yes, we can accommodate most common allergies (e.g., gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free) and dietary preferences (vegetarian, vegan). However, you must inform us at least 48 hours prior to your class so our chefs can prepare the appropriate ingredient substitutions.

Do I need to bring my own ingredients or tools?

No! We provide everything you need. Your station will be prepped with all the premium ingredients, Japanese steel knives, and professional cookware required to complete the menu. Just bring yourself and an appetite.

What is your cancellation policy?

Due to the high cost of premium ingredients prepped specifically for each student, we require a minimum of 72 hours notice for a full refund or to reschedule your class. Cancellations within 72 hours are non-refundable, but you may transfer your ticket to a friend.

The Fine Print

Studio Policies

Arrival Time

Please arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled class time to check in, wash your hands, and get fitted for your apron. Classes start promptly to ensure all recipes are completed.

Alcohol Policy

We provide wine and beer pairings during the dining portion of the evening. Outside alcohol is not permitted. You must be 21+ and present a valid ID to consume alcohol.

Age Restrictions

Standard classes are strictly for adults aged 18 and older due to the use of sharp knives and hot equipment. We do offer specific "Family Baking" classes for children 10+.

Still Have Questions?

If you couldn't find the answer to your question in our FAQ, our support team is standing by to assist you with your booking, dietary needs, or private event inquiries.
