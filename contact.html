Quick Answers

Before You Contact Us

Do you host private events?

Yes! We rent our entire studio for corporate team building, birthdays, and bachelorette parties. Please select "Private Event" in the form above to get a custom quote.

Can I buy a gift card?

Absolutely. Digital gift cards are available and can be applied to any class, membership, or merchandise. They never expire and can be instantly emailed.

What is your cancellation policy?

Because we buy fresh ingredients for every student, we require a minimum of 72 hours notice to cancel or reschedule a class for a full refund.

Is there an age limit?

Standard classes are 18+ due to alcohol service and professional equipment. However, we do host dedicated family and teen baking classes on weekends.
