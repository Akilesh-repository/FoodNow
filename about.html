Our Story & Vision

Where Passion Meets
Culinary Precision.

Founded in 2015, FoodNow was built on a simple belief: everyone deserves to experience the joy of creating restaurant-quality food at home. We are a collective of Michelin-starred chefs, artisan bakers, and culinary educators.

Discover Our Journey
Chapter One

It Started With A Single Recipe.

What began as a small, underground supper club in an old Brooklyn warehouse has blossomed into a globally recognized culinary institute. We noticed that people loved eating fine dining, but were terrified to cook it.

FoodNow was created to bridge that gap. We stripped away the pretension of elite kitchens and built a welcoming, state-of-the-art space where mistakes are just a part of the recipe.

Core Values

Our Culinary Philosophy

We don't just teach you how to follow a recipe; we teach you how to understand food. Our methodology is rooted in three distinct pillars.

Respect Ingredients

Great food starts at the farm. We emphasize sustainable sourcing, seasonal cooking, and utilizing every part of the ingredient.

Master The Science

Cooking is chemistry. We teach the 'why' behind the 'how', from the Maillard reaction to gluten development in doughs.

Express Creativity

Once the rules are learned, they can be broken. We encourage artistic plating and bold, fearless flavor experiments.

"A recipe has no soul. You, as the cook, must bring soul to the recipe."

Chef Thomas Keller

Executive Founder

The Visionary

Decades Of Fine Dining Experience.

After spending 20 years leading the kitchens of Paris and New York's most decorated restaurants, Chef Thomas wanted to build something different. He realized his true passion wasn't just feeding people—it was teaching them.

He designed the FoodNow curriculum personally, ensuring that the same strict standards, hygiene practices, and intricate techniques used in a 3-star kitchen are brought directly to our student stations.
