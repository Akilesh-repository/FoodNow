Billing & Membership FAQ

Can I cancel or pause my membership?

Absolutely. Our memberships are strictly month-to-month. You can cancel, downgrade, or pause your membership at any time directly from your user dashboard without any hidden cancellation fees.

Do unused classes roll over to the next month?

Yes, up to 2 unused classes can roll over to your next billing cycle. If you are on the Executive Chef unlimited plan, rollovers do not apply.

Are ingredients included in the membership cost?

Yes! All tiers include the cost of premium, fresh ingredients required for your scheduled classes, along with use of our professional tools, aprons, and clean-up services.

Can I book a single class without a membership?

Yes, we offer drop-in rates for non-members. Drop-in classes start at $65 per class, depending on the complexity of the menu and ingredients used.
